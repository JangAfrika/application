Please read before you start

  • Write correct data for your child's bio.
  • Have ready: your child's report card (a photo or a PDF) and a phone number for the parent/guardian. An email address is optional.
  • You will pick a payment plan, agree to the terms of enrolment and sign by typing your first and last name.
  • The form must be completed before or within one week of the start date. Failure to return the form may result in the child's unenrolment.
  • Students must come with pen/pencil, exercise books and textbooks (if available).
